Peer Review Policy

All manuscripts submitted to the World Open Research Journal undergo an established peer review process designed to maintain academic quality, research integrity, and scholarly relevance.

The process consists of:

  • Initial Editorial Screening: The editorial board evaluates each submission for relevance to the journal's scope, originality, quality, and compliance with submission guidelines.
  • Double-Blind Peer Review: Manuscripts that pass the initial screening are sent to independent reviewers. The identities of both authors and reviewers are concealed throughout the review process.
  • Editorial Decision: Based on reviewers' reports and recommendations, the editorial board makes the final decision to accept, request revisions, or reject a manuscript.

The journal is committed to maintaining fairness, confidentiality, transparency, and academic integrity throughout the review process.
