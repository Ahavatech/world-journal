Aims and Scope

World Open Research Journal welcomes high-quality scholarly contributions in, but not limited to, the following areas:

Artificial Intelligence and Computer Science

  • Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning and Deep Learning
  • Data Science, Big Data and Predictive Analytics
  • Cybersecurity, Software Engineering and Robotics
  • Cloud Computing, IoT and Information Technology etc.

Engineering and Physical Sciences

  • Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry
  • Civil, Mechanical and Electrical Engineering
  • Computer, Chemical and Petroleum Engineering
  • Energy Engineering, Renewable Energy and Power Technology etc.

Medical, Health and Biomedical Sciences

  • Medicine, Pharmacy and Nursing
  • Dentistry and Oral Health
  • Medical Laboratory and Biomedical Sciences
  • Public Health, Epidemiology and Healthcare Technology

Management, Business and Economics

  • Business Administration and Entrepreneurship
  • Accounting, Finance and Economics
  • Marketing and Human Resource Management
  • Public Administration and Organisational Management
  • Finance, Banking and Investment

Education and Educational Technology

  • Educational Administration and Leadership
  • Curriculum Development and Educational Psychology
  • AI in Education and Digital Pedagogy
  • E-learning, STEM Education and Instructional Technology

Law, Governance and Public Policy

  • International Law and Human Rights
  • Corporate, Environmental and Technology Law
  • Digital Governance and Cyber Law
  • Public Policy and Institutional Development

Digital Humanities and Emerging Technologies

  • Human-Computer Interaction and UX Design
  • Computational Linguistics and Digital Humanities
  • AI Ethics and Philosophy of Technology
  • Digital Communication, Archives and Storytelling
  • Virtual Reality and Data Visualization
