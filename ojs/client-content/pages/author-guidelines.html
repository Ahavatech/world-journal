Guidelines

1. Manuscript Preparation

  • Scope: Manuscripts must align with the Aims and Scope of the journal as outlined on its official website.
  • Formatting: Authors must follow the journal's manuscript template. Submissions should be prepared using Times New Roman font, size 12, and submitted in Microsoft Word (.doc or .docx) format.
  • Language: All manuscripts must be written in English and carefully proofread to ensure grammatical accuracy, clarity, and readability.

2. Submission Process

  • Online Submission: Manuscripts should be submitted through the journal's designated online submission system or official editorial email.
  • Cover Letter: Each submission must be accompanied by a cover letter confirming the originality of the work, its significance, and its suitability for publication in the journal.

3. Article Processing Charge (APC)

  • Publication Fee: The World Open Research Journal charges an Article Processing Charge (APC) of USD 50-100 per accepted manuscript.
  • Payment: Authors will be notified of the applicable APC only after their manuscripts have successfully passed the review process and received an acceptance decision.
  • Purpose: The APC supports editorial and publication operations and the provision of open access to published articles.
  • Non-Refundable Fee: All APC payments are non-refundable, subject to the journal's stated refund policy.

4. Peer Review Process

  • Review Model: All submitted manuscripts undergo a double-blind peer review process, ensuring that the identities of both authors and reviewers remain confidential throughout the review.
  • Review Timeline: The journal aims to complete the initial round of peer review within 5–7 days of submission. However, the actual duration may vary depending on reviewer availability and the complexity of the manuscript.

5. Copyright and Licensing

  • Copyright Agreement: Upon acceptance, authors will be required to complete the journal's applicable copyright and publication agreement.
  • Publication Rights: The agreement will specify the rights granted to the journal for publishing and distributing the accepted article.
  • Open Access: Published articles will be made freely accessible to readers in accordance with the journal's licensing policy.

6. Publication Ethics and Malpractice

  • Originality: Submitted manuscripts must represent original scholarly work and must not be under consideration for publication by another journal.
  • Plagiarism: All submissions may undergo plagiarism screening. Manuscripts containing unacceptable levels of plagiarism or other forms of academic misconduct may be rejected.
  • Research Integrity: Authors are expected to comply with accepted standards of research ethics, proper citation, authorship, and responsible scholarly publication.

7. Post-Publication Policy

  • Open Access: Accepted and published articles will be made immediately accessible to readers.
  • Corrections: Authors who identify errors in their published articles should promptly contact the editorial office. Requests for corrections will be assessed in accordance with the journal's publication policies.

8. Refund Policy

The Article Processing Charge (APC) is non-refundable and non-transferable once payment has been made.

Authors are responsible for ensuring that their manuscripts meet the journal's scope, formatting requirements, and publication ethics standards before making payment.

Refunds will not be issued for manuscript withdrawal, editorial decisions, processing delays, or dissatisfaction with publication outcomes. Any exceptional refund request will be reviewed in accordance with the journal's applicable policies.

9. Research Awards and Recognition Criteria

The World Open Research Journal may recognize outstanding research contributions through its research awards.

  • Originality: Eligible research must demonstrate originality and contribute meaningful knowledge to its respective field.
  • Research Quality: Submissions should demonstrate strong methodology, academic relevance, clarity, and scholarly significance.
  • Outstanding Contribution: Award consideration will be based on the quality and overall merit of a manuscript compared with other eligible publications within the same volume.
  • Selection Process: The journal's reviewers and editorial team will assess eligible manuscripts and determine those deserving of recognition.

10. Manuscript Submission and Contact

Authors are invited to submit their manuscripts through the journal's official online submission platform or editorial email.

For submission enquiries, publication information, or further assistance, please contact the editorial office through the official communication channels provided on the journal's website.
