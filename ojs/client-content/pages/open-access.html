Access and Article Processing Charge (APC) Policy

The World Open Research Journal operates under an open-access publishing model. All accepted and published articles are made freely accessible to readers without subscription or payment requirements.

An Article Processing Charge (APC) ranging from USD 50 to USD 100 may apply to accepted manuscripts, depending on the applicable publication category and editorial requirements. Authors will be informed of the applicable charges following manuscript acceptance.

The APC supports the journal's editorial, production, and publication operations. Payment does not influence editorial decisions or guarantee manuscript acceptance.
