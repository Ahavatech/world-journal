About the Journal
World Open Research Journal (WORJ) is an international, open-access, peer-reviewed, multidisciplinary scientific journal published quarterly. The journal is dedicated to promoting the dissemination of original research articles, review papers, systematic reviews, case studies, and innovative scholarly contributions across diverse fields of science, technology, medicine, engineering, social sciences, management, education, and the humanities.
WORJ provides a global academic platform for researchers, scientists, scholars, professionals, and practitioners to share innovative ideas, communicate scientific discoveries, address contemporary challenges, and contribute to the advancement of knowledge.
The journal welcomes interdisciplinary and multidisciplinary research that explores emerging developments, innovative methodologies, practical applications, and transformative approaches to addressing global scientific, technological, social, and developmental challenges.

JOURNAL POLICIES
1. Disclaimer
The views, opinions, and findings expressed in articles published in the World Open Research Journal are solely those of the respective authors and do not necessarily reflect the views, policies, or positions of the journal, its editorial board, or its affiliated institutions.
The journal does not guarantee the absolute accuracy, completeness, or reliability of information presented in published articles. Authors are responsible for the accuracy and integrity of their contributions. The journal shall not be held responsible for errors, omissions, or consequences arising from the use of information contained in published works.
2. Access and Article Processing Charge (APC) Policy
The World Open Research Journal operates under an open-access publishing model. All accepted and published articles are made freely accessible to readers without subscription or payment requirements.
An Article Processing Charge (APC) ranging from USD 50 to USD 100 may apply to accepted manuscripts, depending on the applicable publication category and editorial requirements. Authors will be informed of the applicable charges following manuscript acceptance.
The APC supports the journal's editorial, production, and publication operations. Payment does not influence editorial decisions or guarantee manuscript acceptance.
3. Peer Review Policy
All manuscripts submitted to the World Open Research Journal undergo an established peer review process designed to maintain academic quality, research integrity, and scholarly relevance.
The process consists of:
Initial Editorial Screening: The editorial board evaluates each submission for relevance to the journal's scope, originality, quality, and compliance with submission guidelines.
Double-Blind Peer Review: Manuscripts that pass the initial screening are sent to independent reviewers. The identities of both authors and reviewers are concealed throughout the review process.
Editorial Decision: Based on reviewers' reports and recommendations, the editorial board makes the final decision to accept, request revisions, or reject a manuscript.
The journal is committed to maintaining fairness, confidentiality, transparency, and academic integrity throughout the review process.
4. Plagiarism and Publication Ethics Policy
The World Open Research Journal maintains a strict policy against plagiarism and all forms of academic misconduct.
Originality: Submitted manuscripts must represent original scholarly contributions and must not be simultaneously under consideration by another journal.
Plagiarism Screening: All submissions may be examined using appropriate plagiarism detection software.
Similarity Threshold: The journal generally accepts manuscripts with a similarity index of no more than 15%, subject to editorial assessment. Properly cited references, standard terminology, and commonly used expressions may be considered during the evaluation.
Violations: Manuscripts found to contain plagiarism, data fabrication, falsification, or other forms of academic misconduct may be rejected or withdrawn from publication.
Author Responsibility: Authors are responsible for ensuring that their manuscripts comply with accepted standards of academic integrity and publication ethics.
5. Privacy Statement
The World Open Research Journal respects and protects the privacy of its authors, reviewers, editors, and readers.
Names, email addresses, institutional affiliations, and other personal information submitted through the journal's website or submission platform will be used exclusively for purposes directly related to journal operations, including manuscript processing, peer review, editorial communication, and publication activities.
Such information will not be sold, shared, or disclosed to unrelated third parties without appropriate authorization, except where required by applicable laws or necessary for legitimate journal operations.
The journal is committed to maintaining the confidentiality and security of all personal information entrusted to it.

GUIDELINES
1. Manuscript Preparation
Scope: Manuscripts must align with the Aims and Scope of the journal as outlined on its official website.
Formatting: Authors must follow the journal's manuscript template. Submissions should be prepared using Times New Roman font, size 12, and submitted in Microsoft Word (.doc or .docx) format.
Language: All manuscripts must be written in English and carefully proofread to ensure grammatical accuracy, clarity, and readability.
2. Submission Process
Online Submission: Manuscripts should be submitted through the journal's designated online submission system or official editorial email.
Cover Letter: Each submission must be accompanied by a cover letter confirming the originality of the work, its significance, and its suitability for publication in the journal.
3. Article Processing Charge (APC)
Publication Fee: The World Open Research Journal charges an Article Processing Charge (APC) of USD 50-100 per accepted manuscript.
Payment: Authors will be notified of the applicable APC only after their manuscripts have successfully passed the review process and received an acceptance decision.
Purpose: The APC supports editorial and publication operations and the provision of open access to published articles.
Non-Refundable Fee: All APC payments are non-refundable, subject to the journal's stated refund policy.
4. Peer Review Process
Review Model: All submitted manuscripts undergo a double-blind peer review process, ensuring that the identities of both authors and reviewers remain confidential throughout the review.
Review Timeline: The journal aims to complete the initial round of peer review within 5–7 days of submission. However, the actual duration may vary depending on reviewer availability and the complexity of the manuscript.
5. Copyright and Licensing
Copyright Agreement: Upon acceptance, authors will be required to complete the journal's applicable copyright and publication agreement.
Publication Rights: The agreement will specify the rights granted to the journal for publishing and distributing the accepted article.
Open Access: Published articles will be made freely accessible to readers in accordance with the journal's licensing policy.
6. Publication Ethics and Malpractice
Originality: Submitted manuscripts must represent original scholarly work and must not be under consideration for publication by another journal.
Plagiarism: All submissions may undergo plagiarism screening. Manuscripts containing unacceptable levels of plagiarism or other forms of academic misconduct may be rejected.
Research Integrity: Authors are expected to comply with accepted standards of research ethics, proper citation, authorship, and responsible scholarly publication.
7. Post-Publication Policy
Open Access: Accepted and published articles will be made immediately accessible to readers.
Corrections: Authors who identify errors in their published articles should promptly contact the editorial office. Requests for corrections will be assessed in accordance with the journal's publication policies.
8. Refund Policy
The Article Processing Charge (APC) is non-refundable and non-transferable once payment has been made.
Authors are responsible for ensuring that their manuscripts meet the journal's scope, formatting requirements, and publication ethics standards before making payment.
Refunds will not be issued for manuscript withdrawal, editorial decisions, processing delays, or dissatisfaction with publication outcomes. Any exceptional refund request will be reviewed in accordance with the journal's applicable policies.
9. Research Awards and Recognition Criteria
The World Open Research Journal may recognize outstanding research contributions through its research awards.
Originality: Eligible research must demonstrate originality and contribute meaningful knowledge to its respective field.
Research Quality: Submissions should demonstrate strong methodology, academic relevance, clarity, and scholarly significance.
Outstanding Contribution: Award consideration will be based on the quality and overall merit of a manuscript compared with other eligible publications within the same volume.
Selection Process: The journal's reviewers and editorial team will assess eligible manuscripts and determine those deserving of recognition.
10. Manuscript Submission and Contact
Authors are invited to submit their manuscripts through the journal's official online submission platform or editorial email.
For submission enquiries, publication information, or further assistance, please contact the editorial office through the official communication channels provided on the journal's website.
